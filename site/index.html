AUTOMATIC PR DETECTION

Every time you log a workout, ActaLog analyzes your performance across multiple metrics. Hit a new max weight? Faster time? More reps? We catch it automatically and celebrate with you.

PR detection works across weight lifted, time to completion, total reps, and rounds completed. When you beat your previous best, you'll see a notification instantly — and your fellow athletes can like your achievement to cheer you on.

ESTIMATED 1RM CALCULATIONS

Not every day is a max-out day — but you still want to know how you're progressing. ActaLog calculates your estimated one-rep max using the industry-standard Brzycki formula based on your working sets.

Log a set of 225 lbs for 8 reps, and we'll tell you your estimated 1RM is around 281 lbs. Track these estimates over time to see your true strength progression without the wear and tear of constant max attempts. Your joints will thank you.

BUILD CONSISTENCY

The best program is the one you actually follow. ActaLog tracks your training streaks, weekly frequency, and attendance patterns to help you build the habit of showing up.

See your current streak displayed prominently on your dashboard. Watch it grow from days to weeks to months. The calendar view shows your training dots at a glance — gaps become obvious, consistency becomes visual proof of your dedication.

Monthly summaries show your total workouts, workout frequency, and how you compare to previous months. Data-driven motivation to keep you coming back.

PRE-LOADED & READY

ActaLog comes loaded with 314 benchmark WODs and 182 movements right out of the box. Fran, Grace, Murph, all the Girls, the Heroes, and CrossFit Open workouts from multiple years — they're all here and ready to log.

The movement library includes Olympic lifts like the snatch and clean & jerk, powerlifting staples like squat and deadlift, gymnastics movements like muscle-ups and handstand walks, and cardio options from rowing to running. INSTALL THE APP

ActaLog is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Install it on your phone for a native app experience — no app store required.

iPHONE / iPAD

  1. Open ActaLog in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap Add to confirm

Note: Must use Safari. Chrome and other browsers don't support Add to Home Screen on iOS.

ANDROID

  1. Open ActaLog in Chrome
  2. Tap the menu (three dots)
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap Install to confirm

Chrome may automatically prompt you to install when you visit the app.

Once installed, ActaLog works offline and launches instantly from your home screen — just like a native app.
