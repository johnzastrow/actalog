SUBSCRIPTION MANAGEMENT

ActaLog includes a flexible subscription system designed for CrossFit boxes. Manage individual memberships or organization-wide access with manual admin control.

INDIVIDUAL SUBSCRIPTIONS

Perfect for drop-in athletes or remote members. Create monthly or annual subscriptions for individual users. Track payment status and expiration dates.

ORGANIZATION SUBSCRIPTIONS

One subscription covers all members of an organization. Ideal for boxes that want to provide app access as part of their membership package.

PERMANENT FREE ACCESS

Grant permanent access to founders, staff, or special members. No expiration, no recurring billing — just full access forever.

MANUAL CONTROL

Mark payments as received, extend subscriptions, or cancel access. You're in control of the billing relationship.

READ-ONLY MODE

Expired subscriptions automatically enter read-only mode. Members can view their history but can't log new workouts.

AUDIT TRAIL

Every subscription change is logged. Know who made changes and when for full accountability.
