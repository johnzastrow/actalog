BROADCAST TO YOUR COMMUNITY

Schedule changes, special events, equipment updates, competition announcements — your members see it all in their notification feed. No more hoping everyone checks the whiteboard or reads their email.

Announcements go directly to every member's notification inbox within the app. They appear with a timestamp and are accessible from anywhere. Members who miss a day still get the message when they open the app.

Write announcements with Markdown formatting for rich text — bold, italics, links, and lists. ActaLog supports standard Markdown syntax so your messages look professional and are easy to read.

MULTIPLE ORGANIZATIONS

Athletes aren't limited to a single community. Users can belong to multiple organizations — their home box, a specialty gym, a competition team, or a remote coaching group.

Each organization has its own admins, announcements, and member lists. Athletes see notifications from all their communities in one unified feed, but workouts and PRs belong to the individual — their data travels with them.

Perfect for coaches who work at multiple locations, athletes who travel frequently, or competition teams that span different boxes.

SUBSCRIPTION MANAGEMENT

ActaLog includes a flexible subscription system designed for CrossFit boxes. Manage individual memberships or organization-wide access with manual admin control.

INDIVIDUAL SUBSCRIPTIONS

Perfect for drop-in athletes or remote members. Create monthly or annual subscriptions for individual users. Track payment status and expiration dates.

ORGANIZATION SUBSCRIPTIONS

One subscription covers all members of an organization. Ideal for boxes that want to provide app access as part of their membership package.

PERMANENT FREE ACCESS

Grant permanent access to founders, staff, or special members. No expiration, no recurring billing — just full access forever.

MANUAL CONTROL

Mark payments as received, extend subscriptions, or cancel access. You're in control of the billing relationship.

READ-ONLY MODE

Expired subscriptions automatically enter read-only mode. Members can view their history but can't log new workouts.

AUDIT TRAIL

Every subscription change is logged. Know who made changes and when for full accountability.

IMPORT, EXPORT & DATA CONTROL

COMPLETE DATA OWNERSHIP

Unlike cloud-only solutions, ActaLog runs on your infrastructure. Your member data never leaves your servers unless you want it to.

Full backup and restore capabilities mean you can migrate, archive, or recover data at any time. The complete database can be backed up with a single click, and restores are just as simple.

GDPR & Privacy Ready

Export member data on request, delete records completely, maintain audit trails for compliance.

EXTENSIVE IMPORT OPTIONS

Migrating from another platform? ActaLog makes it easy. Import your movements, WODs, and workout history from CSV files. Coming from Wodify? We have a dedicated importer that handles their export format directly.

Each import type includes a preview step so you can review what will be added before confirming. Duplicate detection prevents accidental data duplication, and you can choose to skip or update existing records.

COMPREHENSIVE EXPORTS

Your data should always be accessible. Export movements, WODs, and complete workout histories to CSV or JSON formats. Members can export their own data anytime — no admin intervention required.

Admins can generate full system backups that include the complete database. These backups can be used for disaster recovery, migrating to a new server, or archiving historical data.

All exports are timestamped and logged in the audit trail for accountability and compliance tracking.
