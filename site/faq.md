# FAQ

## Can I use PostgreSQL or MariaDB?
Yes — the project supports PostgreSQL and MariaDB. Configure your database settings via environment variables or the `docker-compose.override.yml`.

## Where are sample CSVs and seeds?
See the `seeds/` and `imports/` directories in the repository for `movements.csv` and `crossfit_wods.csv` examples.

## How do I contribute?
Open issues and PRs on the repository. See contribution guidelines in the repo `CONTRIBUTING.md` (if present).
