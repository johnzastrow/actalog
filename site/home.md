## Why ActaLog (consumer‑first benefits)
- **Privacy & control:** Your workouts stay on your infrastructure — no third‑party tracking.
- **Easy to self‑host:** Single-command Docker Compose deployment and optional database choices (Postgres / MariaDB).
- **Fitness features that matter:** WODs, workout templates, movement library, CSV import, and simple analytics.

## Key features (short)
- Log workouts, reps, and weights
- Reusable workout templates and named WODs
- Import / export CSV (seeds available)
- PWA-ready: installable on mobile
- Works with Docker Compose and small VPSes
