BUILT FOR PERFORMANCE

PR TRACKING

Automatic personal record detection for weight, time, and AMRAP workouts. Watch your numbers climb.

PERFORMANCE ANALYTICS

Visualize your progress with charts and graphs. Track movement volume, workout frequency, strength gains.

WORKOUT TEMPLATES

314 benchmark WODs pre-loaded. Create custom templates. Log once, track everything.

QUICK LOG

One-tap logging from templates. Mobile-optimized for gym use. No friction, just results.

IMPORT/EXPORT

Wodify CSV import. Full database backup/restore. Your data, your control.

PROGRESSIVE WEB APP

Install on any device. Offline support. Works like a native app. No app store required.

CLASS SCHEDULING

Full gym scheduling: locations, class templates, sessions, coach assignments, reservations, waitlist, and credit packages.

LEADERBOARDS

PR leaderboards across the gym. Consistency achievements for showing up. Compare your lifts against the community.

MULTI-ROLE ACCESS

Three-tier roles: Athlete, Coach, and Admin. Coaches manage rosters and check-ins. Admins control the full system.
