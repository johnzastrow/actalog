## 1. Introduction

ActaLog ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our workout tracking application.

## 2. Information We Collect

| Data | Purpose | Required |
|------|---------|----------|

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Password hashing | bcrypt with cost factor 12 |
| Transport encryption | HTTPS/TLS required |
| SQL injection prevention | Parameterized queries only |
| Session security | JWT tokens with expiration |
| Account lockout | Automatic after failed login attempts |
| Audit logging | All security events logged |

### 5.2 Your Responsibilities

- Use a strong, unique password
- Keep your login credentials confidential
- Log out from shared devices
- Report suspicious activity immediately

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| Account information | Until account deletion |
| Workout data | Until account deletion |
| Audit logs | 12 months |
| Password reset tokens | 1 hour (then deleted) |
| Email verification tokens | 24 hours (then deleted) |
| Expired session tokens | 30 days after expiry |

### 6.2 After Account Deletion

When you delete your account:
- Personal data is permanently removed
- Workout history is permanently deleted
- Audit logs may be retained in anonymized form
- Backups are purged according to backup retention schedule

## 7. Your Rights

### 7.1 Rights Under GDPR (EU Users)

You have the following rights:

| Right | Description | How to Exercise |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Access** | Obtain a copy of your personal data | Export feature or contact us |
| **Rectification** | Correct inaccurate data | Edit profile in app |
| **Erasure** | Delete your personal data | Contact administrator |
| **Restriction** | Limit how we process your data | Contact us |
| **Portability** | Receive data in portable format | Export feature (JSON/CSV) |
| **Objection** | Object to processing | Contact us |
| **Withdraw Consent** | Withdraw previously given consent | Contact us |

### 7.2 Rights Under CCPA (California Users)

California residents have additional rights:
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt-out of sale (we do not sell data)
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising rights

### 7.3 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights:
1. Use the in-app export feature for data access/portability
2. Edit your profile for rectification
3. Contact the administrator for deletion or other requests

We will respond to requests within 30 days.
